Our story

Munchies After Dark started in Lekki in January 2025. We’re a Lagos cloud kitchen serving burgers, wings, wraps, pasta and desserts from lunchtime to 3 AM.

The Afro brings suya spice to the burger lineup. The MAD pairs breaded cheddar and beef bacon with our house sauce.
